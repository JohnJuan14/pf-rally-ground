COURT ETIQUETTE

THE PADDLE RACK RULES

01

Rack Your Paddle

Slide your paddle into the next open slot in the rack queue. Sets of 4 paddles will enter the court together as the next match opens.

02

Game to 11 (Win by 1)

During peak Open Play, games play to 11 points (win by 1 sudden death) to keep rotation moving smoothly so nobody sits out for long.

03

2-Game Max Rule

Winners may stay on for a maximum of 2 consecutive matches before rotating back to the rack. Tap paddles at the net after every rally!
